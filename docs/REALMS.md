# Twelve provisional realms

These names and mechanics are **design candidates**, not immutable lore or shipped game features. Each realm must tell original stories; inspirations describe mood and player experiences, not copied IP.

| ID | Realm | Surface experience | Local dramatic law | Core playable loop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | The Drift | Moving geography, hidden infrastructure | Observation changes what seems stable | Survey, chart, infer |
| 02 | The Ashlands | Retrofuturist collapse and salvage | Every shortcut has a material cost | Scavenge, craft, negotiate |
| 03 | The Spiral Reach | Starflight, strange civilizations | Distance hides old histories | Explore, ally, decide |
| 04 | Thorncourt | Rival kingdoms and dangerous magic | Promises have force | Investigate, bargain, cast |
| 05 | The Crucible | Ritualized realm contests | Conflict is formalized by rules | Train, duel, arbitrate |
| 06 | The Nocturne | Gothic cyclic horror | Debt outlives its originator | Track, protect, survive |
| 07 | The Breach | Industrial survival and incursion | Boundaries weaken under extraction | Repair, repel, escape |
| 08 | The Storylands | Folkloric towns and moral comedy | Reputation changes opportunity | Befriend, choose, rebuild |
| 09 | Blackglass | Amnesia, noir investigation | Every memory has a witness | Collect clues, interrogate, deduce |
| 10 | The Velvet Mile | Absurd adult social comedy | Misunderstanding compounds | Converse, improvise, recover |
| 11 | Precinct Zero | Grounded procedural investigations | Evidence constrains conclusions | Inspect, connect, report |
| 12 | The Scrap Frontier | Improvised cosmic misadventure | Accidents reward ingenuity | Tinker, puzzle, improvise |

## Shared layer

**The Crossroads** provides potential transit without requiring every game to share a common art style or combat system. The **Department of Everything** claims authority over such travel; its power is a disputed in-world fact. **The Archive of Impossibilities** contains artifacts with anchors, consequences and investigative histories.

## Release discipline

Only the Crossroads lobby is currently playable. Do not label a realm 'implemented' until it has an accessible route, a complete playable loop, persistence checks and automated coverage.
