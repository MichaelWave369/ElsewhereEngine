# Larry, Tweedsie & Mavis: The Unlicensed Trinity

> **Builder. Explorer. Equalizer.** Three mismatched workers, one reality that keeps failing inspection.

**Status:** Original character/side-story bible, public canon candidate in Elsewhere Engine. This document is not a claim of an implemented crossover or universal canon transfer.

## The cast

### Larry — the Builder
A stubborn interdimensional contractor who specializes in mycelium macro-mesh drywall, framing unstable thresholds and patching up structural mistakes caused by people with better titles. Larry's low-key humor hides practical mastery. He has no interest in becoming a chosen one; he wants a fair invoice, uninterrupted weekends and a reasonable lunch break. **Limit:** Larry can fix what he can reach; he cannot decide the ethical cost of sealing a passage.

### Tweedsie — the Explorer
A resourceful, mischievous burrower and familiar resident of the existing original **TweedleVerse** setting. He is curious, communicative through gestures and squeaks, and especially talented at finding what is beneath official blueprints. Not just a chaos button: his discoveries sometimes expose the human stakes of a situation. **Limit:** tunnel routes may breach containment and create problems he cannot repair alone. Any full canonical crossover needs a separately approved TweedleVerse bridge.

### Mavis Plumb — the Equalizer
A seemingly elderly former Department of Everything structural integrity inspector, a practiced witch carrying an old carpenter's level. Retired or terminated? Both records exist. Mavis spots false repairs and knows that the boundary itself might be protecting someone. Her magic requires an **anchor**, incurs a **specific enduring cost**, and cannot erase harm. For the Walbash incident, a brass washer anchors her working; she sacrifices her remembered way home. **Limit:** she cannot waive the cost, and her authority is disputed.

## Relationship engine

- **Larry + Tweedsie:** discovery meets practical repairs. Their escalating mistakes are funny because their friendship is sincere.
- **Larry + Mavis:** "good enough" versus "safe for the people who live here." They disagree and learn from one another.
- **Mavis + Tweedsie:** caution versus curiosity. She is stern because she knows the stakes, not because she dislikes him.
- **All three:** the team can scout a seam, hold it safely and weigh whether it should remain open. No member can do all three jobs alone.

## Pilot case: The Walbash Avenue Job

Location: **StarChucks, 108 Walbash Avenue**, The Crossroads. A mycelium macro-mesh wall accidentally overlays two foundations. The site inspection reveals a disputed dimensional boundary. Tweedsie finds the hidden route; Larry braces the seam; Mavis pays a real cost to stabilize it. The player chooses to seal the gap or retain a monitored, unlicensed passage.

The scene is playable as a **separate offline side episode** at 'trinity.html' with independent saved state. It does not modify Episode 001 or either connected project's data.

## Comedy rule

Comedy comes from mundane people dealing with extraordinary infrastructure, never from erasing the stakes of a cursed place or suffering person. Not every joke is a clue. Sometimes Larry actually just wants lunch.

## Intellectual property boundaries

Original designs, dialogue, art and mechanics only. The cast is not an adaptation of any Office Space, Matrix, Buffy, Rick and Morty or other copyrighted character. Larry is a new fictional contractor. A possible TweedleVerse bridge must be explicitly approved and must preserve that project's characters and story ownership.
